Current predicaments aside, this world is a indeed, a very strange place to live in. On one hand, it is such a bliss to be alive, to be filled with the morning rays, yet, at the same time, we feel a pathetic lethargy to even leave the bed in the wee hours of morning. Not from the sleepy eyes, as one might expect, but the mere fact that we have to face the world, yet another day. We have to go out and interact in this world, once again. Same at night, on one hhand, we have the beauty of the mmoon, or better, a moonless clear sky. But on the other hand, nighttiem brings a strange nostalgia, or is it melancholy, for what could have been. Or worse, being deep down the rabbit hole of searching the answers for *why*. Indeed, world is such a beauty stitched through contradictory ideas.
